READY— FPS

W accelerates. S brakes, then reverses. A and D steer. Escape pauses. On touch screens, drag the steering wheel and hold the gas or brake pedal. If the car rolls over, press R or use the Right the car button that appears next to the car chooser. The edges are connected. The same instructions are painted on the ground and listed in the settings menu.
